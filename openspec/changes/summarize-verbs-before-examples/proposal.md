# Explain the lexical item before choosing examples

## Why
Verb preparation shows Ready and then asks the learner to choose a sentence
without first showing what the verb means. Word preparation already has a framed
summary. Reuse that renderer instead of introducing another preview style.

## What Changes
Show a framed dictionary summary for verbs after meaning selection and before
example selection, morphology packages, or picture preparation. Show the target,
verb type, selected Russian gloss, and available IPA. For an encountered form,
label its infinitive, infinitive meaning, and infinitive IPA explicitly.
Keep the existing word summary before examples. Use English interface headings
and row labels in the shared summary; learner meanings and explanations remain
Russian. Render only available data, without extra generation or inferred grammar.
Existing cards, output routes, and confirmations are unchanged.
