## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Visual image-search scenes

Image search SHALL favor concrete visual scenes matching the selected sense. Abstract concepts MAY use recognizable metaphors and English search phrases. Classification SHALL match complete words or phrases rather than substrings within unrelated words.

#### Scenario: Trust as a visual metaphor
- **WHEN** an image is sought for Vertrauen in the sense of trust
- **THEN** suitable searches include visual scenes such as a trust fall or a child holding a parent's hand, rather than document-filling queries.
